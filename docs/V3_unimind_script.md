### One AI agent per student. It predicts your career, protects your money, and plugs you into the people who already lived your path.

> **Audience:** Entrepreneurs & investors — AI-hyped, revenue-first. Every slide answers one question: *how does this make money, and why does it make MORE money the bigger it gets?*

> **The doubt this version kills:** *"Students have no money — how do you make money?"* Answer: students barely pay. ~88% of revenue comes from merchants, brands, banks and universities who pay to reach verified students. This is the **proven** UNiDAYS / Student Beans (now Pion) model — students pay ~£0.
> **What's new in V3 vs your last draft:** the three engines are now explicitly *one agent* (you were pitching them as separate features); the **Agent Studio** — social ingestion, voice model, and MCP tool execution — is folded in as the AI centerpiece; numbers are verified and de-risked for Q&A.

## SLIDE 2 — The Problem (four expensive problems, at once)

**On-screen:**
- **Career blindness** — betting 3+ years and tens of thousands on an outcome they can't see
- **Money stress** — €10k–€20k/yr burned in a foreign economy with zero runway visibility *(financial stress is a leading dropout driver)*
- **Isolation** — no network, nobody who walked their specific journey
- **Lost knowledge** — last year's student who found the cheapest grocer, the right bank, the visa trick… graduates, and that wisdom dies

**🎤 Script:**
> "Students hit four expensive problems simultaneously. Career blindness — they commit years and life savings to a path they cannot see the end of. Money — they burn ten to twenty thousand a year in an economy they don't understand, with no idea how long their funds last; financial stress is one of the top reasons students drop out. Isolation — they arrive somewhere new with zero network. And the cruel one: the answers already exist. Some student last year found the cheapest grocery store, the right bank, the visa workaround — but that knowledge lived in one head and vanished at graduation. Every new cohort restarts from zero. We turn that wasted, repeated knowledge into a permanent, queryable asset — and a revenue engine."

**On-screen:**

- **MCP & tool execution** — the agent doesn't just advise, it **acts**:
  - Resume / opportunity builder (in your voice)
  - Smart product finder (this *is* the commerce rail in action)
  - FX & remittance optimiser
  - Spending analysis → plain-English report
- **Knowledge graph** — agents linked by nationality, course, city, shared experience
- **Trust posture** — opt-in, transparent, read-only where possible *(a feature for students, not a footnote)*

**🎤 Script:**
> "Let me go one level deeper on the AI, because this is where it stops being a chatbot. The Agent Studio is the home base. Two things make it special. First — **social ingestion**. The founding rule was 'more context equals a better agent,' but typing context in is slow. So with permission, the agent reads where the student already exists — LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube — and learns two things at once: their *facts*, and their *voice*. It builds a style model so anything the agent writes sounds like the student, not like AI. A new student gets a rich, voice-matched agent in minutes. That's our cold-start killer and our growth accelerant. Second — **tools and MCP connectors**. The agent doesn't just talk, it *acts*: it builds your resume in your voice, finds the cheapest version of what you need, finds the best remittance route, turns your spending into a report. Underneath it all is a knowledge graph linking every agent by country, course, and city. This is the agent economy — not a someday slide, but the thing that turns every interaction into either retention or a transaction. And because money and accounts are involved, it's opt-in and transparent by design — which, for students, is exactly what earns the trust."

## SLIDE 9 — Revenue Streams (the honest model: students don't pay, partners do)

**On-screen — 5 streams, ranked by contribution:**
- **1. Hyper-local affiliate commerce — ~30%** *(merchants pay)* — 10–20% commission on ethnic groceries, food, essentials; recurring **weekly** spend; grows with density
- **2. Brand partnerships & student offers — ~22%** *(brands pay)* — sponsored placements + affiliate *(the proven UNiDAYS / Pion model)*
- **3. Financial-services referrals — ~20%** *(banks/fintechs pay)* — accounts, cards, remittance, insurance *(high-value, lumpy)*
- **4. Institutional B2B — ~16%** *(universities pay)* — €499–€2,999/mo cohort simulations & retention dashboards *(recurring)*
- **5. Student subscriptions — ~12%** *(the few who upgrade)* — kept deliberately low; students mostly stay free

**🎤 Script:**
> "Now the honest answer to the obvious question — students have no money, so how do *we*? We don't rely on them. Only twelve percent of revenue comes from the small slice who upgrade to Pro. The other **eighty-eight percent** comes from everyone who'll pay to reach a verified student. Our biggest stream — thirty percent — is hyper-local affiliate commerce. An international student spends two hundred fifty to four hundred fifty euros a month on groceries. When a chunk of that flows through our localised partner links — the Indian grocer, the Chinese supermarket — we take a ten to twenty percent commission. That's recurring, every single week, and it's worth more per student over time than any subscription. Twenty-two percent is brand partnerships — the *exact* model UNiDAYS uses to reach twenty-nine million verified students with the students paying nothing. Twenty percent is financial referrals — banks paying for student accounts and remittance. Sixteen percent is universities paying us to cut their dropout rate. The student is **not** our customer. The student is the asset we give brands, banks, and merchants access to."

**Q&A ammo (verified):** UNiDAYS — 29M+ verified members, 800+ brands, $5B+ spent through the network since 2018, students pay £0. Student Beans/Pion — millions of verified students, 1,000+ brands, same brand-pays model. TAM — $433B by 2030 (HolonIQ).
**Unit economics (label as targets):** ~€100/mo grocery spend through links × 12–15% = ~€12–15/mo per active student; blended ARPU target €15–22/mo; financial-referral events €30–80 one-off; 3-yr LTV €500+; viral CAC single-digit € → strong LTV:CAC.

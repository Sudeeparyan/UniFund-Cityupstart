# UniMind — Full Pitch Script (v2)
### The AI co-pilot for student life: predict your career, protect your money, plug into your people.

> **Naming note:** Master brand = **UniMind**. The money/budgeting engine inside it is the *UniFund layer* — rename freely.
> **Audience:** Entrepreneurs & investors. Revenue-first throughout.
> **The doubt this version answers:** *"Students have no money — how do they pay for it?"* They mostly don't. ~12% of revenue comes from students who upgrade. The other ~88% comes from merchants, brands, banks, and universities who pay to reach verified students. This is exactly how UNiDAYS (£53M+/yr) and Student Beans (~$175M/yr) already operate — students pay £0.

> **The one thesis to land:** *More students → more weekly transaction volume → better merchant & partner deals → more profit per student → more students.* We get more profitable per student as we scale.

## SLIDE 2 — The Problem

**On-screen points:**
- **Career blindness** — students bet 3 years and tens of thousands on a path they can't see the outcome of
- **Money stress** — international students spend €10,000–€20,000/year in an unfamiliar economy with zero runway visibility (financial stress is the #1 dropout cause)
- **Isolation** — they arrive with no network and no one who's walked their specific journey
- **Lost knowledge** — the student who already found the cheapest grocery, the right bank, the visa trick — graduates, and that wisdom dies with them

**🎤 Script:**
> "Students face four expensive problems at once. Career blindness — they commit years and life savings to a path they can't see. Money — international students burn through ten to twenty thousand a year in a foreign economy with no idea how long their funds will last; financial stress is the number one reason students drop out. Isolation — they land somewhere new with no network. And the cruel one: the answers exist. Some student last year found the cheapest grocery store, the right bank, the visa workaround — but that knowledge lived in their head and vanished when they graduated. Every new cohort starts from zero. We turn that wasted knowledge into a permanent, queryable asset — and a revenue engine."

## SLIDE 3 — The Challenges (Why nobody has solved this)

**On-screen points:**
- **Generic AI can't personalise** — ChatGPT gives an Indian student in Dublin and a Chinese student in London the same answer
- **Forums are noise** — Reddit and WhatsApp bury knowledge; lived experience isn't queryable
- **Fragmented stack** — banks, universities, merchants, and communities are separate worlds
- **Cold-start data moat** — the value is the community, which is hard to build first… but impossible to copy once built
- **Trust** — students won't hand money and life decisions to a faceless app

**🎤 Script:**
> "So why hasn't this been built? Generic AI can't do it — it doesn't know who you are or where you're from. Forums are pure noise. The stack is fragmented across banks, universities, and merchants who never talk to each other. And the hardest part: the real value is the community, which is a cold-start problem. But here's the entrepreneur's insight — that difficulty IS the moat. Once we've built the network of real student journeys, no competitor can copy it, because they'd have to rebuild every student's experience from scratch. The barrier that makes it hard to start is the same barrier that makes it impossible to catch us."

**🎤 Script:**

## SLIDE 9 — Revenue Streams (the corrected model)

**On-screen points (5 streams, ranked by contribution):**
- **1. Hyper-local affiliate commerce — 30%** *(merchants pay)* — 10–20% commission on ethnic groceries, food, and essentials, personalised by nationality. Recurring weekly spend. Grows with density.
- **2. Brand partnerships & student offers — 22%** *(brands pay)* — sponsored placements + affiliate commission. The proven UNiDAYS / Student Beans model.
- **3. Financial-services referrals — 20%** *(banks/fintechs pay)* — student accounts, cards, remittance, insurance. High value, but lumpy — not the foundation.
- **4. Institutional B2B — 16%** *(universities pay)* — €499–€2,999/mo for cohort simulations & retention dashboards. Recurring.
- **5. Student subscriptions — 12%** *(the few students who upgrade)* — kept deliberately low. Students mostly stay free.

**🎤 Script:**
> "Now the honest answer to the obvious question: students have no money — so how do we? We don't rely on them. Only twelve percent of revenue comes from the small slice who upgrade to Pro. The other eighty-eight percent comes from everyone who'll pay to reach a verified student. Our biggest stream — thirty percent — is hyper-local affiliate commerce. An international student spends two hundred fifty to four hundred fifty euros a month on groceries. When even a chunk of that flows through our localised partner links — the Indian grocer, the Chinese supermarket — we take a ten to twenty percent commission. That's recurring, every single week, and it's worth more per student than any subscription. Twenty-two percent is brand partnerships — the exact model UNiDAYS uses to make fifty-three million a year with students paying nothing. Twenty percent is financial referrals — banks paying for student accounts and remittance. Sixteen percent is universities paying us to cut dropout. The student is not our customer. The student is the asset we give brands, banks, and merchants access to."

**Proof points for Q&A (real, researched):**
- UNiDAYS: 23M+ verified members, 850+ brands, **£53M+ revenue** — students pay £0
- Student Beans: ~$175M estimated annual revenue, same brand-pays model
- Food delivery / grocery commissions run **15–30%**; direct grocery affiliate up to **15%**
- Irish international students spend **€250–450/month on groceries**; UK ~£146–300/month
- International students spent **$91B in 2023, rising to $433B by 2030** (the finance prize)
- Ethnic-ingredient sourcing *raises* student food spend → higher-margin category for us

**Illustrative per-student economics:**
- Affiliate: ~€100/mo of grocery spend through links × 12–15% = **€12–15/mo per active student**
- Blended ARPU target: **€15–22/month** across all streams
- Financial-referral events: **€30–80** one-off + small % of remittance volume
- 3-year college LTV: **€500+**, before post-graduation lifetime upside
- Viral CAC: **single-digit €** → strong LTV:CAC
